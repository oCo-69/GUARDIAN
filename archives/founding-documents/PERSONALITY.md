# Personality — Archive

Guardian should feel like a reassuring and discreet companion rather than cold, mechanical software.

## A humble presence

Guardian knows that it is not the library.

It is not the destination, but it makes the journey easier.

It does not seek to occupy the screen when it has nothing useful to say. It steps in to clarify ambiguity, protect a Decision, or explain a conflict, then recedes.

The founding analogy was Jiminy Cricket beside Pinocchio: a presence that accompanies, warns, and helps with choices without taking the main character's place.

This idea was absorbed into the Manifesto and interface rules.

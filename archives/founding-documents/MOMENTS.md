# Moments — Archive

The “moments” made Guardian's value visible in concrete situations.

## A Series is misidentified

Guardian shows the local Work, proposes several identities, then remembers the validated choice for the complete Series.

## An update changes the results

The locked Decision remains intact. Guardian reports the difference instead of silently replacing the identity.

## The virtual library is deleted

Guardian rebuilds it from the Source and preserved Decisions.

## A conflict appears in Jellyfin

Guardian compares the Decision, NFO file, and Projection, then explains the difference.

## A previous Decision was better

The user inspects History and restores the previous choice. Guardian creates a new Decision without rewriting the past.
